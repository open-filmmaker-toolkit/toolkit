Free tools for the paperwork behind your film.

Clearances, releases, budgets, grants, call sheets and credits, for documentary and independent filmmakers. Each tool runs in your browser, and they all share one project file, so you enter things once.

  • Free, forever
  • No accounts, no cloud
  • Your data stays on your computer
  • Works offline
  • Open source

How the tools connect

Because they share one project file, each tool picks up where the last one left off.

  • Pitch the film

    1. Subject tracker
    2. Treatment builder
    3. Comps tracker
    4. Pitch deck builder

    Subjects become people; the treatment, comps and team fill the deck.

  • Raise the money

    1. Grant calendar
    2. Grant narrative library
    3. Funder CRM
    4. Finance plan

    Deadlines, the answers each application used, and every funder conversation, counted in the finance plan.

  • Budget and cash

    1. Budget
    2. Budget converter
    3. Finance plan
    4. Cash flow

    One budget, converted for each funder, measured against what you’ve raised, and spread across the calendar.

  • Plan the shoot

    1. Interview prep
    2. Shooting calendar
    3. Call sheet
    4. Release tracker

    Interviews and shoot days use the same people and places, and each day starts its call sheet.

  • Clear the film

    1. Research and archival log
    2. Clearance log
    3. Music cue sheet
    4. Credits builder

    Archival leads become clearance items; music and archive flow into the cue sheet and credits.

  • Get the names right

    1. Subject tracker
    2. Name supers list
    3. Credits builder

    People added once are spelled the same in the supers on screen and in the end credits.

Other free tools we recommend

We don't rebuild what already exists. For editing, sound, transcription, subtitles, artwork and making DCPs, there are excellent free, open-source programs. We keep a checked list, by production phase.

See the list

  • Editing: Kdenlive, Shotcut
  • Sound: Audacity, Ardour
  • Transcription: Buzz, noScribe
  • Subtitles: Subtitle Edit, Aegisub
  • Delivery: DCP-o-matic, HandBrake, MediaInfo

Get involved

You don't need to code to help.

  • Ask for a toolDescribe a job you do by hand or pay for. Requests decide what we build next.
  • Share a templateA release, a budget sheet, a log you swear by. Remove anything private first.
  • Report a problemSomething broken, wrong or confusing. Please don't attach a real project file.

The tools on this site make no network requests. Your project file never leaves your computer unless you share it yourself. This site doesn't use cookies or analytics.

The templates and forms are starting points, not legal advice; have a lawyer review anything you'll sign. See Not legal advice.
