# Using the toolkit offline

Every tool is a single web page with nothing to install. There are two ways to use it without an internet connection.

## Install it from the web

Open the [toolkit's home page](https://open-filmmaker-toolkit.github.io/toolkit/) once while you're online. The browser keeps a copy of the home page and every tool on your device, and the home page says when that's done. After that, the toolkit opens without a connection.

To give it its own window and icon:

- **Chrome and Edge:** click the install icon at the right of the address bar, or **Install the toolkit** at the bottom of the home page.
- **Safari on a Mac:** choose **File > Add to Dock**.
- **Safari on an iPhone or iPad:** tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**.
- **Firefox:** Firefox doesn't install web apps, but it still keeps the copy, so the pages open offline in the browser.

## Updates

While you're online, each page you open is the newest version, and the copy on your device is updated with it. When a new tool is published, the browser saves it too the next time you open the toolkit online. There's nothing to update by hand.

## Your project stays yours

Installing changes nothing about your data. The copy the browser keeps is of the toolkit's own pages, not your project. Your project is still the `.oft.json` file you open and save, and nothing is sent anywhere.

## Or download it

You can also [download the repository](https://github.com/open-filmmaker-toolkit/toolkit/archive/refs/heads/main.zip), unzip it, and open `index.html` or any tool's `index.html` from your computer. That works offline as it is, but doesn't update itself.

To remove the installed copy, uninstall the app, or clear the site's data in your browser's settings.
