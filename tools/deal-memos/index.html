        Rates and rules

        Fill these in from your union or guild agreement, or from what you agreed with the crew. Timecards use the rules; deal memos warn when a rate is below the minimum for its role.

        Overtime, meals and turnaround

        Hours worked are from call to wrap, less the meal. Rest cut short is paid at the overtime rate. A weekly rate counts as five days.

        Timecards

        This works out hours and pay from the daily reports so you can check timecards. It doesn't replace a payroll service: it doesn't withhold taxes, pay anyone or know every rule in your agreement. Hand the summary to your payroll company and let them run the numbers.
