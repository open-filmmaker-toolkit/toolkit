Build the budget account by account: each line is a quantity, a unit and a rate, with fringes on payroll lines. Contingency, insurance and fees are percentages on top. Totals roll up to the top sheet as you type.

    Part of the Open Filmmaker Toolkit. This page makes no network requests: everything stays on this computer until you save or export it. Uses the same project file as the other toolkit tools.

    Account

    Fringes and percentages

    Fringes

    Payroll taxes, benefits or union contributions, as a percentage of the lines you apply them to. Pick a fringe on each payroll line.

    Percentages on the whole budget

    Contingency, insurance, fees and overhead, as a percentage of all lines plus fringes. Check the rates your funders and insurer allow.
