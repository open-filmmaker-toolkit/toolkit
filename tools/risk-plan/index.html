    Plan for what could go wrong on a shoot: each hazard with how likely it is, how bad it would be and what you'll do about it, plus emergency contacts, the nearest hospitals, how you'd get out, and a check-in schedule with what to do when someone misses one. Print one copy for the crew and one for your insurer.

    This tool helps you write a plan down; it doesn't replace training or professional advice. For hostile environments, get hostile-environment training and talk to your insurer and a security adviser before you go.
