    Key

    Sold a signed deal holds the right. On hold a deal is being negotiated. Part sold a deal holds some languages or part of the territory. Held back another deal keeps it off the market until a date. Available nobody holds it.

    Overlaps

    Exclusive deals that cover the same territory, media and language at the same time, and deals that start before another deal's holdback ends.

      Reverting soon

      Signed deals that end in the next six months, when the rights come back to you.
