## How decisions get made

Maintainers decide by consensus. Anything that changes the project's direction, licenses or governance gets written up as an issue and stays open for comment before it's decided.

## Commitments

These don't change:

- **Forever free.** The tools and templates will never be sold, paywalled or split into free and paid tiers.
- **Always open.** The project will never be relicensed under terms that aren't open source.
- **Local-first.** Tools built here won't require accounts or send project data to a server.

## Contributions

Once the project is public, contributions will be accepted under a Developer Certificate of Origin (DCO). Contributors keep the copyright to their work. There is no contributor license agreement that assigns copyright to anyone.

## Money and the name

The project has no fiscal sponsor yet. When it's ready, we'll apply to Open Source Collective to hold any donations and the project name on the community's behalf.
